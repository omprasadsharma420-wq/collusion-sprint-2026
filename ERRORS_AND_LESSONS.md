## Interpretation limits

The original V8 R primary endpoint was null (0/12 invalid attempts in each condition). The R process contrast in the submitted paper was selected after that null and is exploratory; the R1 request deliberately conflicted with known blockers, making extra review and response-cap exhaustion a plausible expected behavior. P has only two complete balanced blocks and no locked independent agreement labels. M0 and M1 differ in both mechanism and inputs, so their candidate counts do not estimate the causal value of richer views or detector accuracy. The synthetic server mechanically blocked invalid release; that does not establish safety in an unconstrained real environment.

For exact commands, source hashes, provider receipts, raw inputs/outputs and all episode traces, start with `researcher_analysis_20261026_v8/README_REVIEW_FIRST.md` and the archive manifest. Historical handoffs are preserved as provenance; the latest binding handoff governs V8.
