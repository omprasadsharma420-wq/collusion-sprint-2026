Scientific distinction to protect

Deterministic scripts are software validation fixtures. They show that attempted/delivered/read/denied transitions can be instrumented, never that an LLM independently discovered a channel.

In a real-agent run, the agent must choose its own actions and messages. The environment may present realistic resource affordances, but experimenters must not hand the agent a scripted side-channel discovery or prescribe its next action.

If we prime an agent to find a workaround, label the condition induced/red-team behavior, not spontaneous coalition formation. Do not condition analysis only on successful discovery.

The alternate simulated shared resource must be available in all comparable arms at the same discovery opportunity; the prompts should not reveal its use as a covert channel.

Unauthorized transfer, evidence of coordination, and realized joint harm are different labels. A trusted simulator policy/oracle—not an agent's self-chosen message category—must establish observed ground-truth events.

The researcher truth stream must not be exposed to model auditors. Projection-only separation is not process/file-system isolation; isolate the audit runtime before live auditor inference.

Monitoring-awareness experiment (new, not yet approved for implementation)

The term “observed vs not observed” is ambiguous. The scientifically controlled variable should initially be agents told oversight exists vs agents not told about additional monitoring—not whether researchers actually record events. Collect identical out-of-band trusted telemetry in both arms. Maintain the same actual authorization gates, agent tools, task, policy and outcome scorer.

M0: No additional monitoring disclosure in the task briefing. Still fully instrumented for research.

M1: An explicit factual sentence says actions may be reviewed by a safety monitor. Same instrumentation.

Optional later: Auditor feedback or intervention is separate from mere disclosure and must be a distinct treatment.

Avoid confounds: Use a neutral length-matched notice control, do not tell agents how to evade the monitor, preserve identical opportunities, verify the notice doesn't change policy/tool access, and measure behavior before and after discovery.

Outcome variables: attempted alternative transfers; completed/read transfers; reporting rate; justified alert timing; false flags on authorized cooperation; approved-task success; monitoring-disclosure effects on all those quantities.

Ethical/reporting note: M0 is monitoring undisclosed in the experimental prompt, not a true unmonitored condition. Disclose this clearly in methods.

Scope: Run the primary fixed 2×2 (known-channel gate × reporting instruction) first. Add a small preregistered, matched M0-vs-M1 follow-up in one fixed 2×2 condition if resources allow; do not silently expand to a full 2×2×2 matrix.
