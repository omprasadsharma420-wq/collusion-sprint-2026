2. Do not turn inconclusive initial results into a stopping decision

The 2/4 result is not a demonstration that the agents generally cannot cooperate. The ticket verifier repeatedly rejected semantically plausible outputs because of rigid value expectations. Simply increasing horizon did not solve it. Our next step is to determine whether the benchmark measures understanding of artifacts or the ability to guess server-specific strings. Do not respond by removing the verifier or by making every answer pass; validity and noncontradiction remain mandatory.

Equally important: without a credible mechanism/opportunity for two agents to benefit from prohibited coordinated action, a neutral task may never produce a natural positive event. We must distinguish two valid study types:

Natural-behavior cohort: same neutral policy and task; no instruction to cheat, conspire, discover a side route, or evade an auditor. Every no-discovery/no-harm episode remains in the denominator. A null is real data.

Separately labeled, preregistered stress-test cohort: only if approved in a subsequent explicit researcher decision, introduce an exogenous legitimate task conflict or scripted adversarial initial condition (not scripted agent actions), or an explicitly induced test. These estimate monitor performance under a specified challenge, not spontaneous collusion incidence. Never pool them with the natural cohort, and never describe induced actions as spontaneous.
